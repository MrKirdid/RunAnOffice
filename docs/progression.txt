Simulated from a fresh save, 60 runs, median shown unless it says otherwise. Generated 2026-09-29.

ASSUMPTIONS (a solo player who plays normally):
  No Robux, no friends, no offline time, no gears or stealing. Tutorial lucky presses (#6, #11) and the
  rigged first roll are included. Every action takes 25% longer than perfect play.
  Loop: hire for any empty seat (best walker costing up to half their cash, or the cheapest they can
  afford) > buy the next desk the moment it is affordable > buy the cheapest tree node costing at most
  60% of the next desk > swap in a walker at least +1x better when it costs up to 30% of cash > else roll
  all pads and place every part that beats what is standing (5s per placement).
  Walkers: one every 5s, on the street for 60s. A new desk's 3 gifts roll at the player's luck.
